# Roadmap, validation hypotheses, and future ideas

No dated release plan, commercial target, validated demand, or historical roadmap
commitment was recoverable. This roadmap is a **Derived recommendation**, not a
promise to deliver the listed future capabilities.

## Suggested sequence

| Stage | Outcome | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Resolve product baseline | Agreed scope and coherent lifecycle/contact rules | Current decisions, requirements and open-question register |
| Select implementation and operations | Stack, license, owners, data lifecycle and support matrix | Explicit technical/operational decisions |
| Build the smallest adopted loop | Discover, host, join/leave, reconfirm, public contact controls and service notices | Product acceptance scenarios run against implementation |
| Pilot across Spain and UK cities | Observe whether people can find and fill suitable matches | Measured results and qualitative feedback |
| Iterate on demonstrated needs | Address the highest-impact observed failures | New decisions, hypotheses and updated scope |

The pilot covers all cities in Spain and the UK; stages do not silently narrow it
to a chosen city shortlist. Any sport is supported, including custom sport input.

## Candidate success measures — Proposal

- Time from discovery to a successful join.
- Fraction of published matches reaching the intended playing capacity.
- Drop-off at sign-in, skill declaration, role selection and join completion.
- Reconfirmation completion/failure after time/location changes.
- Withdrawals and cancellations, without inferring fault or introducing penalties.
- Whether players can coordinate when nobody publishes a contact.
- User understanding of public contact visibility and hide controls.
- Reported match-data errors and operational failures.

Targets, measurement windows, expected volume and analytics consent/data design
are Open. These are proposed aggregate measures; they do not authorize collecting
raw contacts or copying external conversation content.

## Research hypotheses

Instant joining may reduce waiting but can create capacity and reliability
challenges. External chat may simplify MVP scope but moves coordination outside
the app. Public contacts allow coordination before joining while requiring clear
owner publication controls. Immediate release on rescheduling may refill matches
quickly but can displace previous players. In-app-only notices may be missed by
people who do not return.

These are reasoning-based hypotheses, not findings from user research.

## Future ideas

**Explicitly deferred from MVP:** private games, waitlists, recurring games, ratings,
payments and venue booking (DEC-042), plus host transfer (DEC-022).

**Unapproved ideas:** teams/leagues, tournaments, reputation, automatic matching,
social feeds, calendar integrations, native mobile clients and richer notifications.

Native participant chat was explicitly excluded from MVP; reconsidering it later
requires a new decision. The other ideas are not claimed as previously discussed,
rejected permanently, or committed post-MVP work. MVP is free to use; future
monetization and expansion remain uncommitted under [Q-018](open-questions.md#q-018).

## Change discipline

Add a proposal with user need, trade-off, measurable outcome and scope impact.
Adopt it only with evidence of a product decision. Preserve superseded records and
update requirements, journeys, validation scenarios and changelog together.
